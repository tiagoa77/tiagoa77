### 🧑‍💻 About me

I'm **Tiago Augusto**, **Integration Tech Lead at MC Digital** in Portugal.

I lead the **integration platforms**, the layer that lets applications, systems and partners exchange data reliably, securely and at scale. My work covers platform ownership and governance, integration architecture, technical leadership of the team, and turning business needs into flows that run in production.

**Platforms I'm responsible for:**

- 📡 **Streaming & Messaging**: Apache Kafka and Solace event brokers
- 🚪 **API Gateways**: Gravitee and Google Apigee
- 🔗 **Oracle SOA Suite**: service orchestration and enterprise integration
- 🗄️ **Oracle Data Integrator (ODI)**: data integration and ETL/ELT

**What I focus on:**

- 🧭 Integration architecture, standards and best practices
- 📈 Reliability, observability and scalability in production
- 🔐 Secure API exposure and lifecycle management
- 🤝 Bridging business, product and engineering teams

I also take on the occasional freelance web project.
